**The short version**

- Squish Sort has **no accounts**. Your progress, collection, coins, settings and any names you type stay **on your iPhone**. We never receive them.
- The game asks your age once, and keeps only an age group (child, teen or adult) and, for players 13 and over, a minimum age between 13 and 18. It never keeps your birth date or birth year.
- Ads are shown by **Google AdMob**. Google receives technical data such as your IP address and device information to show and measure ads. Ads are only **personalised** for players old enough to agree **and** who tap Allow on Apple's tracking prompt (and, in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, also agree in Google's consent form).
- For players 13 and over (14 and over in Korea), the game sends **game statistics** (such as levels played and features used) to **Google Analytics for Firebase**, linked to a random app ID and never to your name. It is never switched on in gentle mode, waits for your agreement in the UK, EEA, Switzerland and Korea, and can be turned off in Settings.
- Players under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and anyone who skips the age question, get **gentle mode**: child-safe, non-personalised ads, no tracking prompt, no tracking and no game statistics.
- **Purchases** go through Apple. We never see your card or Apple Account details.
- If you write to support, we use your email only to reply.
- We do not sell personal data.
:::

## What stays on your iPhone

These are saved only on your device. We do not receive them, and they are deleted when you delete the app:

- Game progress, stars, levels completed and settings (such as sound).
- Your squishy collection, spares, capsules opened and coin balance.
- Names you give your Squishy Buddy or squishies you make in the Squishy Workshop, and anything you draw there. Names are filtered for rude words. Names and drawings never leave the device.
- Your age group (child, teen or adult), for players 13 and over a minimum age between 13 and 18 (for example "at least 15"), how it was set (Apple's age range, the age question, a parent's choice or a skipped question) and your App Store country at the time. **Your year of birth is used once to work out the group and is not stored.**
- A random install number, used only to make daily puzzles and lucky squishies the same each time you open the game. It never leaves the device.
- Your answer to the Buddy's "How are you today?" question is not stored at all.
- Records of purchases you made (the product and Apple's transaction number), so a purchase is only counted once.

iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) may include this game data. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

## Game statistics (analytics) {#analytics}

To find out which levels are too hard, where players stop and which features they enjoy, the game uses **Google Analytics for Firebase**. The rules:

- **Off until the age question.** Nothing is sent before the age step. It is switched on only for players 13 and over (14 and over in Korea). It is **never switched on in gentle mode**, for a skipped age question or for an unknown age. Switching to gentle mode later turns it off and resets its data on the phone.
- **Asks first in the UK, EEA, Switzerland and Korea.** There it stays off until you agree on the privacy choices screen.
- **You can turn it off at any time** in **Settings > Privacy choices > Share game statistics**. That stops collection and resets the random app ID on the phone.
- **What is never sent:** names you type, your age or birth year, your email or any account, Apple's advertising identifier or anything else used for tracking. We don't set a user ID, and Analytics' advertising features are switched off.
- **Who sees it:** Google processes the statistics for us as our processor, under Google's data processing terms. We have switched off Firebase's settings that let Google use the data for its own purposes. We see only reports, not individual players' names, because we don't have them.


## Ads, tracking and your choices

- **Players under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and anyone who skips the age question:** every ad request is marked as directed to children. Google then shows only non-personalised, general-audience (G-rated) ads and must limit its data use under children's privacy laws. Apple's tracking prompt is never shown.
- **Teens under the age where personalised ads may be offered:** 18 in the UK, US, Canada and Australia; 14 in Korea; 16 in some EU countries; otherwise the local age of consent (13 in Japan, Taiwan, Hong Kong and Singapore). Below that age, ads are non-personalised and requests are marked as under the age of consent.
- **Everyone else:** at the start of level 7, the game may show Apple's prompt asking to allow tracking. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, you still get ads, but they are not personalised and the IDFA is not shared. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Google's consent form also appears, after the age question. You can change your answer at any time in **Settings > Privacy choices** in the game (shown in the UK, EEA and Switzerland), and in iPhone **Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**.

Google's own policies explain how it uses data from apps that show its ads: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). More detail is on our [ads and tracking page]({{h}}/ads).

## Children's privacy

Squish Sort is designed for a mixed audience, including children. We follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the GDPR rules for children and Korea's rules for under-14s. In short:

- The ads and game statistics systems do not start until the age question is answered or skipped. Before that, the game only fetches its settings file from our website and the product list from Apple, which involve an IP address as any internet request does.
- A child's age group is set to gentle mode. Unknown or unclear ages are treated as a child.
- We do not collect children's names, contact details, photos, voice, precise location or any other information that identifies them.
- For children, the only data that leaves the device for ads is what Google AdMob needs to run child-directed ads: the IP address, an app-specific device identifier, basic device and app information, and which ads were shown or tapped. It is used only for these internal operations: showing contextual (non-personalised) ads, limiting how often an ad repeats, counting ad views and clicks, preventing fraud and keeping the service secure, and meeting legal duties. We make sure of this by marking every ad request from a child as child-directed, which switches off personalised advertising, remarketing and third-party ad vendors at Google, by never asking for Apple's advertising identifier, by never switching on game statistics (analytics) for children, and by not adding any other analytics or advertising tools. It is not used to contact a child, to build a profile or for behavioural advertising. Google keeps this data under its own retention policy (see "How long we keep data"); we keep none of it.
- Children are asked to pass a grown-up check (a sum most young children can't do) before making purchases or leaving gentle mode. There are no pop-ups pushing purchases in gentle mode.
- If a child writes to support, we use the message only to reply once (ideally to a parent) and aim to delete it within 30 days of answering.

**Parents' rights:** you can ask us at any time what information we hold about your child, ask us to delete it, and refuse further collection. Because we hold nothing on our servers about players, deleting the app removes all game data. To stop all collection, delete the app. Write to privacy@squishsort.app with any question. See also the [parents' guide]({{h}}/parents).

We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the conversation. ## The website

- **No cookies.** This website does not set cookies.
- **Hosting:** our host, Vercel, processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect the site, and keeps short-lived server logs.
- **Visitor statistics:** we use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you or following you across sites. It sees the page, the referring site, your browser, device type and country, and a hash of your IP address and browser that resets every day.
- **Your browser remembers** a couple of small preferences on your device only (for example, whether the demo's sound is muted). See [cookies on this website]({{h}}/cookies).
- **App Store badge:** once the game is out, the "Download on the App Store" badge is loaded from Apple's servers, so Apple receives your IP address when the page loads.
- **Squishy Shop:** the page asks for a year of birth to check you're a grown-up. It is checked in your browser and never sent or stored, apart from a "passed" note kept until you close the tab. Links to shops such as Amazon are affiliate links. If you click one, that shop receives your visit and may use its own cookies under its own privacy policy.
- **App Store links:** links to the App Store carry a short campaign label naming the page you clicked from, so we can see which pages lead to downloads. It doesn't identify you.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (for example, by turning off tracking). Because we keep no player data on our servers, most requests are answered by deleting the app, but write to privacy@squishsort.app and we will answer within one month (or sooner where the law requires). We may need to confirm it's really you, without asking for more data than necessary.

**You can object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests**, by emailing privacy@squishsort.app. We make no automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the CNPD in Luxembourg, the ICO in the UK or your local authority in the EU.
