**The short version**

- Squishy Workshop **collects no data**. On the App Store it is labelled **Data Not Collected**.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code** (no SDKs) of any kind.
- **No accounts and no sign-in.** Children never type their name or anything else. Squishy names come from a spinning name wheel.
- The squishies a child makes **stay on the device**. We never receive them. A photo of a drawing is turned into a squishy on the device and the photo itself is never kept.
- The app does not use the internet, except for Apple's own purchase system.
- There is **one optional purchase**, "Unlock everything", a one-time purchase behind a grown-up check. Apple handles the payment; we never see who bought it.
:::

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no social features and no accounts. It contains no third-party code that collects data. It does not use the photo library, microphone, contacts or location, and it does not use the device's advertising identifier.

## The camera: a photo of a drawing

A child can turn a drawing on paper into a squishy by taking a photo of it. The camera is used only at that moment, and everything happens on the device. The photo itself is never stored or sent anywhere: only the cut-out drawing is kept, with the squishy that was made. If the photo shows a person's face, it is refused and nothing is saved.

## What stays on the device

The squishies your child makes, their names, the room they live in and the app's settings are saved only on the device. They never reach us or anyone else. If a later version offers saving to iCloud, that copy will go to your family's own iCloud account, which we cannot see, and we will update this page before that version is released.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

## The purchase and the grown-up check

The app is free to try. "Unlock everything" is a one-time purchase that opens every part of the app. It sits behind a grown-up check, so a young child cannot reach it by tapping around. Family Sharing is on, so one purchase works for the whole family.

Payments, Ask to Buy and refunds are handled by Apple under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see your name, card or Apple Account. Apple gives us only sales totals that do not identify anyone. To restore the purchase on a new device, open the Grown-ups corner and tap Restore purchases.

## Links

The Grown-ups corner links to this page and to our support page. Both sit behind the same grown-up check and open in the browser, outside the app. There are no ads in the app.

## Children's privacy

Squishy Workshop is made for children aged 5 and under and is in the App Store's Kids category. Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing to ask a parent's consent for under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR, the UK GDPR and Age Appropriate Design Code, or similar laws.

## If you write to us

If a parent or carer emails us, we use the email address and message only to reply, and keep them as described in the [Squish Sort privacy policy]({{h}}/privacy#support). Please don't send us a child's personal details.

## Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we will update this page and its date before the change reaches the app. We will never start collecting data from children.
